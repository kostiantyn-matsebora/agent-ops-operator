--- layout: default --- {%- comment -%} The landing layout. Every word on this page comes from the page's own front matter or its body — the hero line, the stat tiles, the strip below them. This file decides only how those are arranged, which is what keeps the theme free of product prose. The product visual is NOT here. It is the first panel of the page's own tab strip, so its alt text, its caption and the manifest beside it are the page's words — the layout would otherwise own content, which is the one thing it may not. Which is also why the stat tiles are placed by SPLITTING the page's content at its first heading rather than by a marker the page writes: everything before the page's first section is its opening, the tiles close that opening, and the sections follow. The page states its words in order and says nothing about where the tiles go. {%- endcomment -%} {%- comment -%} The hero is real text, not part of the drawing: the site's diagram page has its masthead removed precisely so the headline is selectable, translatable, and readable by a screen reader without going through alt text. {%- endcomment -%}
